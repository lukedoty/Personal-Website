December 24th, 2024

It is December 24th, 2024, and I have just taken a shower. In that shower I was thinking about how the last few days have felt all jumbled together with little sense of passing time. Also with little sense of progression, or momentum, or traction. I think that I should write. A little bit (at least) every day. I have this little start of a personal website, and though it isn't yet live to the world, I should like to keep my writing here. The writing could be casual or formal. I don't want to prescribe a strict style to anything, as that sort of decision paralysis is what typically kills this sort of project for me. Anything can go. I think the format of this website works particularly well for that.

I've formatted this page like, well, a page. The size is akin to an A5 piece of paper. Fits pretty well on a screen. More pages for the same day will go over there -> to the right. Future days will go down and down and down. At least for now.

A brief intermission, merry Christmas! It just turned past midnight into the 25th, though I will keep writing as it it's the 24th. Days are more about contiguous awake periods rather than dates.

Am I writing to an audience? I don't think I'm looking for one. Who are you? My future self? Someone who has stumbled onto this page? Perhaps a make-believe collection of little internet creatures living inside my computer. Perhaps I make up a character to write to, like an imaginary pen-pal. I like that idea, though I will put it on hold for lack of a satistying name.
